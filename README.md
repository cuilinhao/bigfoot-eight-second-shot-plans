# Bigfoot eight-second shot plans

A small, reusable prompt and storyboard kit for fictional Bigfoot/Sasquatch shorts. Each example is built around one visible action so an eight-second clip has a clear beginning, middle, and end. You can adapt the prompts in [Bigfoot Studio](https://bigfootvideogenerator.com/) or use the shot plans as writing exercises with another video workflow.

## Quick method

1. Choose one location, one action, and one camera move. Avoid trying to fit a full chase or multi-scene plot into eight seconds.
2. Pick landscape (16:9) for a wide scene or portrait (9:16) for a vertical short.
3. Describe the subject and action before style words. Add lighting, weather, and sound only when they help the shot.
4. If starting from a still image, describe motion that plausibly follows the image. If starting from text, anchor the character and setting in the first sentence.
5. Generate, watch the result, and change one variable for the next pass. A prompt is a direction, not a guarantee of exact timing, speech, or anatomy.

## Blank prompt template

Copy this and replace the brackets:

> An eight-second [portrait/landscape] video of a fictional Bigfoot in [specific setting and time of day]. Bigfoot [one clear action]. The camera [one camera position or move]. [One lighting detail]. [One ambient sound]. End with [simple visual payoff]. Natural motion, consistent character appearance, no scene change.

| Time | Storyboard beat | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 s | Establish subject and location | Is Bigfoot recognizable at a glance? |
| 2–6 s | Show one action | Can the movement be followed without a cut? |
| 6–8 s | Land on a small payoff | Does the final frame make sense without text? |

## Four ready-to-edit examples

### 1. Misty forest greeting

**Format:** portrait, text to video. **Intent:** a gentle reveal with no jump scare.

**Prompt:** An eight-second portrait video in a misty pine forest at sunrise. A friendly fictional Bigfoot steps from behind a tree, notices the camera, and gives one small wave. Handheld eye-level camera stays mostly still. Warm light passes through the fog; distant birds are audible. End on the wave, with the character fully in frame. One continuous shot.

**Storyboard:** 0–2 s: tree and silhouette; 2–6 s: one step into light; 6–8 s: wave and hold. **Revision lever:** if the face is unclear, bring the camera closer instead of adding another action.

### 2. Campsite coffee

**Format:** landscape, image to video when you already have a campsite frame.

**Prompt:** Animate the supplied campsite image as one eight-second landscape shot. Keep the tent, trees, and Bigfoot in their starting positions. Bigfoot raises a metal mug, takes a careful sip, and lowers it with a contented nod. Static medium-wide camera, dawn light, quiet fire crackle. End with the mug near the chest. No new characters or cutaways.

**Storyboard:** 0–2 s: hold the starting composition; 2–6 s: lift and sip; 6–8 s: lower and nod. **Revision lever:** if the mug changes shape, simplify to Bigfoot holding it while turning toward the fire.

### 3. Snow track discovery

**Format:** portrait, text to video. **Intent:** reveal scale through a single object.

**Prompt:** An eight-second portrait video on a quiet snowy trail. A fictional Bigfoot kneels beside one oversized footprint and brushes loose snow from its edge with a hand. Low camera at track level slowly pushes in. Soft overcast light, light wind in the trees, no dialogue. End with the footprint and hand visible together in the frame. One continuous shot.

**Storyboard:** 0–2 s: establish track and figure; 2–6 s: brush snow once; 6–8 s: push in on the footprint. **Revision lever:** if the footprint is hard to read, use a side angle with stronger shadow.

### 4. Creek crossing

**Format:** landscape, text to video. **Intent:** a simple bit of physical comedy.

**Prompt:** An eight-second landscape video of a fictional Bigfoot crossing a shallow forest creek. Bigfoot takes one cautious step onto a flat stone, slips slightly, then regains balance with arms out. Wide locked camera shows the entire body and both banks. Bright afternoon light, flowing water sound. End with Bigfoot standing safely and looking back at the stone. No cuts, no other people.

**Storyboard:** 0–2 s: approach the stone; 2–6 s: step and wobble; 6–8 s: recover and look back. **Revision lever:** if the motion is too complex, remove the look back and end on the recovery.

## Review checklist

- Is the fictional creature visible in the first two seconds?
- Is there only one main action and one camera move?
- Do the opening and final frame tell the same small story?
- Are the sound and setting compatible with the scene?
- If using a starting image, do you have the right to use it?

These are creative starting points. Generated video can differ from the written plan, so review the output before publishing or reusing it.
